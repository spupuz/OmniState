## 2025-02-15 - Fix `<main>` Landmark Boundaries
**Learning:** In dashboards, the `<main>` tag must encompass all unique primary content (like stat grids and bottom panels) so skip-links don't accidentally bypass critical metrics or leave other key grids orphaned.
**Action:** Always ensure the `<main>` tag begins before the first piece of unique interactive or informational content and closes after the last section before the footer.
